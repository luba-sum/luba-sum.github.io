Journal Paper: In Preparation

Analyzing Operational and Safety Impact of Encroachments Through Video Analytics for Heterogeneous Traffic

In preparation

AuthorsFahim, B., Tabassum, L., Doha, H. H. K., Hossain, M., Raihan, M. A., Quddus, M.
Journal version of the TRB 2026 paper.
VenueTarget journal: Accident Analysis & Prevention
StatusIn preparation
ProjectOperational and Safety Impact of Encroachments in Heterogeneous Traffic

Abstract

Encroachment, a pervasive challenge in developing megacities like Dhaka, severely compromises traffic safety, indicated by critical Time-to-Collision (TTC) events, and operational efficiency, evidenced by reduced traffic flow and space mean speed. Notably, computer vision has not been leveraged to quantify the precise impact of encroachments on urban traffic, particularly in locations with heterogeneous traffic. This study aims to identify and quantify the effects of various urban roadway encroachments on traffic safety and operational efficiency, and to evaluate potential improvements from their removal. Utilizing an RF-DETR (Roboflow Detection Transformer) detection model on video data from four key locations in Dhaka (Mirpur, Uttara, Banani, and Board Bazar), we extracted vehicle trajectories to predict critical Time-to-Collision (TTC) events. Comparative traffic simulations in SUMO, without encroachments, allowed for analysis of TTC event probabilities, vehicle flow, and space mean speed. Our findings indicate a significantly high probability of 21% of critical TTC events compared to 3% and markedly lower vehicle flows (61% reduction) and space mean speeds (34% reduction) in traffic with encroachments. These results underscore the urgent need for targeted urban planning and stringent enforcement strategies to mitigate encroachments, thereby significantly improving road safety and traffic efficiency in urban centers.

Abstract as in the conference version: 105th Annual Meeting of the Transportation Research Board (TRB 2026), Washington, D.C..

Keywords: Encroachment; Heterogeneous traffic; Video analytics; Computer vision; RF-DETR; Time-to-Collision (TTC); Operational efficiency
